# Police database & ALPR misuse — documented cases

Research agent report, 2026-09-01. For a "has it happened here?" page.
Caveats: Project South and Reesor are unproven allegations; note "charges
untested in court" wherever used.

## Ontario disciplinary cases (general database misuse)

- **Const. Jerome Rabiha-Stevens (Ottawa, 2026):** pleaded guilty to 4 CSPA
  misconduct counts — **~69 unauthorized searches over a year+** in police
  and MTO databases: plates of ex-partners, a woman he was seeing, women he
  met at the gym, members of the public. Once confronted his then-partner
  in a parking lot on duty and backed his cruiser into the other man's car.
  Outcome: demoted 1st→2nd class for 18 months (OPAAC consent order, Apr
  2026). https://www.cp24.com/news/canada/2026/04/24/ottawa-police-officer-demoted-after-69-unauthorized-database-searches-into-ex-partners-members-of-public/
  Decision: https://www.policearbitration.gov.on.ca/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/ADJ-26-013-Ottawa-Section-202-Demotion-and-Termination-of-a-Police-Officer-Cst.-Rabiha-Stevens-April-20-2026-Consent-Ord.pdf
- **Const. Andrew Reesor (Ottawa, charged 2026, pending):** alleged **77
  unauthorized searches on 44 women (incl. his sister), 27 men, 6
  colleagues** in an MTO database; admitted motives of "curiosity" and
  "attraction." OPS seeking termination. Chief publicly warned officers to
  "change your behaviour or quit." https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/ottawa/ottawa-police-officer-accused-of-unauthorized-database-searches-into-women-colleagues-9.7181538
- **Toronto Police (Globe and Mail review, Feb 2026):** Gaudino (2018,
  Versadex/CPIC/IntelliBook searches on acquaintance); **Collymore (2018,
  licence-plate query "no justification")**; Smith (2019, contacted DV
  victim from prior case); Price (2022, docked 8 days' pay, repeat
  offender re: ex-partner); Gomez (2024, criminal conviction, leaked data,
  house arrest). https://www.theglobeandmail.com/canada/article-toronto-police-division-at-core-of-major-project-south-probe-has/
- **Const. Corkill (TPS, 2024):** CPIC queries on a prospective tenant;
  prior history. https://leca.ca/wp-content/uploads/CORKILL-Hearing-and-Penalty-Decision-dated-February-19-2024.pdf
- **Hampel v. TPS (2009, Div. Ct.):** CPIC search of wife's vehicle;
  misconduct upheld — CPIC "not exclusively for police business."
- **RCMP (ERC summaries):** D-078 — resignation ordered for CPIC queries
  helping gang members counter a murder investigation; C-045 — accessed
  files to get phone numbers of two women (one a minor) for personal
  contact. https://www.canada.ca/en/rcmp-external-review-committee/services/case-summaries.html

## The tow-truck scandals (databases as a commodity)

- **Ottawa 2020:** 3 constables charged (RCMP anti-corruption) with selling
  collision/location info to tow operators; two resigned in exchange for
  stayed charges, admitting "multiple officers" sold tips. Tow owner
  charged with obtaining access to police databases.
- **OPP 2020–22:** ~8 officers charged across towing probes; 4 pleaded
  guilty to breach of trust.
- **Const. Roberto Visconti (OPP):** referred **700 of 703 tows** to two
  GTA operators, shared schedule and radar locations; ~$925K benefit;
  guilty of breach of trust (2024), fired 2026. https://www.cp24.com/local/toronto/2026/06/04/ontario-police-officer-who-gave-preferential-treatment-to-two-gta-tow-operators-fired/
- **Project South (Feb 2026, charges untested):** 27 charged incl. **7
  active TPS officers + 1 retired** — accused of selling confidential
  police-database information to a towing-rooted criminal network; leaked
  data allegedly used for extortion and shootings. **IPC Ontario opened its
  own investigation** into unauthorized police use/disclosure; Commissioner
  Kosseim: "zero tolerance" under new CSPA powers.
  https://www.ipc.on.ca/newsrelease/information-and-privacy-commissioner-of-ontario-to-investigate-unauthorized-use-and-disclosure-of-personal-information-by-police/

## Canadian ALPR-specific misuse

**None documented publicly.** All Canadian plate-misuse cases run through
CPIC/MTO/RMS lookups, not ALPR scan databases. Closest are systemic: BC
OIPC F12-04 (2012, retention unlawful); BC OIPC 2023 (VicPD/Saanich staff
snooped on an FOI requester). **The absence is the finding:** either
Canadian ALPR audit trails catch nothing, or nobody is looking — the US
record shows what turns up when someone reads the audit logs.

## Oversight statistics

- **OPC 2011 RCMP audit:** CPIC 200M+ queries/yr; **280 reported CPIC
  breaches in 2009**; only ~10% of breaches found by audit activity;
  PROS lacked account monitoring; MOUs missing for ~25% of agencies.
  https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/opc-actions-and-decisions/audits/ar-vr_rcmp_2011/
- No published Ontario-wide count of police-snooping complaints found —
  supports our FOI aggregate-statistics ask.

## US benchmarks

- **AP (2016):** 2013–2015, **325+ officers fired/suspended/resigned** for
  database misuse + ~250 lesser discipline; stalking an ex was the common
  pattern; AP called it "unquestionably an undercount."
- **Minnesota DVS:** ex-officer Anne Marie Rasmusson looked up **500+
  times by officers across 18+ agencies**; $1M+ settlements; 2013 auditor:
  "more than half" of MN law-enforcement personnel had made questionable
  queries.
- **Kansas Flock case:** Sedgwick KS Police Chief Lee Nygaard searched his
  ex-girlfriend's plate **164 times** and her new boyfriend's 64 times
  (2023), logging fake reasons ("missing child," "narcotics
  investigation"); resigned; certification revoked; **no criminal
  charges**. Found via Flock audit logs (404 Media). https://www.404media.co/tag/flock/
- **Pattern by 2026:** at least **~50 documented cases** of officers using
  Flock/ALPR to track romantic interests/exes, incl. multiple chiefs.
  https://www.404media.co/cops-keep-getting-arrested-for-using-flock-to-stalk-people/
