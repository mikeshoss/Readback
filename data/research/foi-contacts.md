# MFIPPA submission channels — five Ontario police services

Verified from official sites, 2026-09-01. No service routes MFIPPA through
its Police Services Board — all go to a unit inside the service. No service
offers a verified email-submission channel for a complete request.

## Toronto Police Service
- Unit: Information Privacy Section (page also says "Information Protection & Access")
- Mail: Toronto Police Service, Information Privacy Section, 4th Floor, 40 College Street, Toronto, ON M5G 2J3
- Portal: NONE — mail or in person only (HQ lobby, 7:00–16:30 Mon/Tue/Thu/Fri, closed Wed; 416-808-7850)
- Fee: $5 cheque/money order payable to "Toronto Police Service"; debit/Visa/MC in person only
- Form: "Access/Correction Request" PDF from https://www.tps.ca/services/freedom-information-request/
- Notes: fee estimate if >$25; 50% deposit if estimate ≥$100

## Halton Regional Police Service
- Unit: Freedom of Information Unit
- Mail: Halton Regional Police Service, Attn: Freedom of Information Unit, 2485 North Service Road West, Oakville, ON L6M 3H8
- Portal: https://forms.haltonpolice.ca/Freedom-of-Information-Request-Form (Mastercard/Visa online)
- Fee by mail: cheque — payee NOT stated on page; confirm at 905-825-4777 ext. 5192
- Source: https://www.haltonpolice.ca/en/about-us/freedom-of-information.aspx

## Peel Regional Police
- Unit: Information and Privacy Unit (attn: Freedom of Information Coordinator)
- Mail: Information and Privacy Unit, Peel Regional Police, 7150 Mississauga Road, Mississauga, ON L5N 8M5
  (CAUTION: old Brampton HQ address 7750 Hurontario St still circulates in stale listings — do not use)
- Portal: https://peelpolice.onlinepolicecheck.ca?primessp=FOI
- Fee: cheque payable to "Peel Regional Police"
- Email IPU@peelpolice.ca exists but only in video-request context — not a verified FOI submission channel
- ID: two pieces of government ID, one photo. 45 business days stated. 905-453-2121 ext. 4263
- Source: https://www.peelpolice.ca/en/services/information-and-privacy-requests.aspx

## York Regional Police
- Unit: Freedom of Information Unit
- Mail: Freedom of Information Unit, York Regional Police, 47 Don Hillock Drive, Aurora, ON L4G 0S7
- Portal: https://www.yrp.ca/freedom-information-request-form (Visa/MC online)
- Fee by mail: cheque/money order payable to "York Regional Police"
- Email foi@yrp.ca published as contact — full email submission not explicitly accepted
- Form (mail): YRP436 .docx from yrp.ca. One photo ID. Up to 45 business days. 1-866-876-5423 ext. 6740
- Source: https://www.yrp.ca/en/about/freedom-of-information.asp

## Waterloo Regional Police Service
- Unit: Access to Information Unit (their name for it)
- Mail: Access to Information Unit, Waterloo Regional Police Service, P.O. Box 3070, 200 Maple Grove Road, Cambridge, ON N3H 5M1
- Portal: Microsoft Forms link from https://wrps.ca/en/about-us/freedom-of-information.aspx (unusual platform but official)
- Fee: $5 by mail — payment method/payee NOT specified; confirm before mailing
- Email foi@wrps.on.ca published as contact (old .on.ca domain); full email submission not clearly supported
- Mail package: request form + photocopy of one ID + $5. 45 business days.
