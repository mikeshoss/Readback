its Police Services Board — all go to a unit inside the service. No service
offers a verified email-submission channel for a complete request.

# Expanded filing routes — verified 2026-09-05

Second research pass, covering operators on our camera map that are not
police services, plus the major forces we have not yet approached. Every
field below comes from the organisation's own page; "NOT STATED" means the
official page is silent, not that the search failed.

## Three cross-cutting traps

**1. Ontario is 45 business days, and many pages are stale.** ontario.ca:
"Starting July 1, 2026, the time to respond to FOI access requests changes
from 30 calendar days to 45 business days." Applies to FIPPA and MFIPPA.
Durham and Hamilton have updated. **Brampton, Chatham-Kent, Sault Ste.
Marie, Windsor and Ottawa still publish "30 days" and are wrong.** Do not
calculate a deemed-refusal appeal date from their pages.

**2. The payee is a trap in four cases.** Cheque to the wrong name comes
back and the clock never starts:
- Windsor Police → **City of Windsor**
- Ottawa Police → **Treasurer of the City of Ottawa**
- Edmonton Police → **City of Edmonton**
- Any Ontario ministry (MTO, OPP via SolGen) → **Minister of Finance**

**3. Alberta FOIP no longer exists.** Replaced by the Access to Information
Act (ATIA) and Protection of Privacy Act, effective 11 June 2025. Calgary
and Edmonton both cite ATIA now.

Fees vary by jurisdiction: Ontario $5 · federal $5 · UBC (BC) $10 ·
Calgary and Edmonton $25 · Quebec no flat fee, per-page instead.

## 407 ETR — not accessible, and why

407 ETR Concession Company Limited is a private consortium under a 99-year
concession signed 1999. **It is not in Ontario's Directory of Institutions
and there is no access-to-records process.** Its privacy notice says "both
PIPEDA and FIPPA apply to our collection, use, and disclosure of Personal
Information" — that is a statement about privacy obligations, not a
concession that it is an institution subject to FIPPA *access requests*.
The only route it offers is an individual accessing their own personal data.

**File with MTO instead.** The Province is the counterparty to the
concession and holds contract, oversight and correspondence records about
407 ETR, including potentially its camera and tolling infrastructure.
Expect third-party commercial-information exemptions.

⚠️ **VERIFICATION GAP — do not publish this to readers yet.** We could not
retrieve the Highway 407 Act, 1998 to check whether it expressly applies or
disapplies FIPPA to the concessionaire (CanLII 403; ontario.ca e-Laws
returned an empty shell). The claim above rests on 407 ETR's own privacy
notice plus its absence from the Directory. Confirm with the IPC of Ontario
(1-800-387-0073) or read s. 1–5 of the Act before this goes on the site.
