Draft content piece (user-provided). Candidate for a site feature page:
each official claim paired with the vendor's own documentation.

> Not Flock. Axon Fusus. Everything below on the right is from Axon's own
> product docs and press releases.
>
> **"It reads licence plates."**
> The fixed cameras also do vehicle attribute recognition — colour, make,
> model — and livestream. It's a camera you can watch, not just a scanner.
>
> **"Reads are deleted after 7 days."**
> Retention is a dropdown under Agency Settings. Axon's own default is
> 30 days. Changing it is an admin permission, not a board vote.
>
> **"Data is never sold to third parties."**
> Correct, and beside the point. Axon's ALPR Sharing lets partner agencies
> search your data. Sharing isn't selling.
>
> **"No facial recognition."**
> That's a software setting, not a hardware limit. Axon paused face
> recognition in 2019 and resumed evaluating it in 2025 — they're now
> field-testing it on body cams with Edmonton Police.
>
> **"Only limited, trained members can search the data."**
> Permissions are agency-configured. Whoever holds ALPR System
> Administration can change the retention period and the permissions.
>
> **"Audit logs are stored indefinitely."**
> Axon's own docs flag a known limitation in the sharing audit report: two
> key fields are hardcoded placeholders. It can tell you a sharing config
> changed, not what changed.
>
> None of this means anyone is lying. It means every safeguard is a
> setting, and no one outside the service checks what the settings are.
